# APPENDIX: METHODOLOGY

## ICP Score Calculation
ICP Score = (0.6 x CvR_normalized) + (0.4 x AvgARR_normalized)
Index = ICP Score x 100

Where:
- CvR_normalized = Segment CvR / Overall CvR (43.3%)
- AvgARR_normalized = Segment Avg ARR / Overall Avg ARR ($26.4k)

## Classification Thresholds
- PRIORITISE (^): Index > 110 (more than 10% above overall average)
- NEUTRAL (--): Index 90-110 (within 10% of overall average)
- DEPRIORITISE (v): Index < 90 (more than 10% below overall average)

## Minimum Sample Thresholds
- FTE Bands: 5 opportunities
- US States: 3 opportunities
- Industries: 5 opportunities
- Company Types: 5 opportunities
- Revenue Bands: 3 opportunities

## Data Sources
- CRM: Clay export of Salesforce closed opportunities for Your Product
- Gong: Recorded sales calls filtered to the configured product line
- Amendment record types excluded (contract changes, not new acquisition)
- Empty/null values excluded from averages

## Data Limitations
- No Win/Loss Reason field in CRM dataset
- No GRR or Profit Margin fields in CRM dataset
- Gong pain_points and objections are free-text (not structured tags)
- Competitor data from Gong is based on what reps logged (may be incomplete)
